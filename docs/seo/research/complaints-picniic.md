# Picniic — Complaint Research

## Overview

Picniic ("Family Organizer by Picniic," Picnic Labs Inc., App Store id 1039586709) was an all-in-one family hub app — shared calendar, to-do/shopping lists, secure storage for sensitive family info, location sharing, and a meal planner with recipe search — that received notable press (CBS, Forbes, USA Today, Time) around its 2016 launch. **Status: discontinued.** The app is no longer available on the App Store (lookup returns 0 results) and the company site no longer functions. No pricing data recoverable — product is defunct.

This is a "graveyard" competitor: its main research value is as a cautionary/trust data point (a well-funded, press-covered family app that shut down and stranded users) and as a source of feature-set language people still search for.

- **Problem:** A previously well-reviewed, press-covered family organizer app shut down entirely — site non-functional, app delisted — leaving any household that had built their routines around it with no migration path or data export.
