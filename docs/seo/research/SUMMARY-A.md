All underlying quotes are verbatim from Apple App Store customer-review RSS feeds (US storefront, pages 1-2, fetched 2026-09-15) unless marked as a third-party blog paraphrase. Reddit was not accessible in this research pass (see individual files for detail) - this table is therefore App-Store-review-weighted, not a full social-listening picture. ## Strongest recurring signal

Two clusters dominate: paywalls on basic functionality (all four competitors, in different flavors) and forced/unwanted AI with data used for training (concentrated in TimeTree - five reviewers used explicit "deleting the app" language within about six weeks). Both map directly onto stated Kinrows differentiators (free core, no ads, no data sale, no training on user content, Concierge strictly opt-in). App reliability (crashes, data loss, broken invite flows) is the third major theme, concentrated in FamilyWall and TimeTree.
