# AnyList — Complaint Research

## Overview

AnyList (by Purple Cover, Inc., App Store id 522167641) is a long-running, well-regarded grocery-list and recipe-manager app: shared shopping lists that sync in real time, recipe import/organization, and meal planning. Free with a paid Complete/Pro tier (recipe scaling, meal planning calendar, etc.) — exact current price not confirmed in this research pass. Strengths: mature product, strong recipe-import feature, long track record, widely recommended as "the" shared grocery list app. It is grocery/recipe-only — no calendar, budget, pantry-expiry, chores, or family-chat features, which bounds its comparison relevance to Kinrows' Lists/Cook areas specifically.

## Complaint clusters

### 1. Real-time sync reliability breaking the core "shared list" promise
- **Problem:** The single reason people choose AnyList — instant list sync between household members — periodically breaks, causing duplicate purchases and wasted money.
- **Quotes (App Store US RSS, https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/id=522167641/sortBy=mostRecent/page=1/json, accessed 2026-09-15):**
  - "We've used this app for a long time but are growing frustrated with syncing issues that have arisen during the past few months. Despite all kinds of troubleshooting, our grocery list won't sync like it used to. This results in duplicate purchases and wasted money: Purchased items are crossed off the list on one phone but still appear as unpurchased on the other. AnyList, please fix this!" — vtamoeba, 2★, "Syncing not reliable," 2026-07-25
  - "I share a grocery list with another member of the family. We put a long list into his phone. Then hours later, when I went to look at the list on my phone, it had not yet crossed over.. So we opened his list up again, so I could transcribe it into my phone and his list flickered and disappeared. All that work for nothing!" — 2thomps, 1★, "This app has a major glitch!," 2026-08-22
  - "Updates will occasionally erase list memory" — The GrimRocker, 1★, "Frustrating," 2026-07-25
- **User type:** Long-time couples/families using it as the household grocery list.
- **Intent:** Bug report → frustration-driven alternative search.
- **Related Kinrows capability:** Lists feature — server-validated, household-scoped shared lists with automatic grocery categorization.
- **Candidate page type:** "AnyList not syncing" troubleshooting-to-comparison post; "best shared grocery list apps."
- **Conversion potential:** High — this is a trust-breaking, "one job" failure.

### 2. Losing Alexa / smart-home voice integration
- **Problem:** AnyList removed or degraded Alexa voice integration and the iOS lock-screen widget, making hands-free "add milk to the list" workflows disappear.
- **Quotes:**
  - "Not blaming AnyList but it has become useless in my house which is totally Alexa ecosystem and on my devices which are all Apple. My number one reason for having it was for the ease of asking out loud for items to be added to a list... Hate to rate it this low but it is useless." — Phil Corcoran, 1★, "Incompatibility Problem," 2026-08-15
  - "This app is pretty well worthless if I can't integrate it with Alexa" — Iowabearhawks, 1★, "Alexa gone..," 2026-08-22
  - "No use for this app with the removal of the lock screen widget. Used to be my go to for glancing at my shopping list but now, with that feature gone, it serves no useful purpose. May as well use my notepad" — Dezboi, 2★, "iOS Lock screen widget removed," 2026-08-31
- **User type:** Voice-assistant / widget-dependent grocery shoppers.
- **Intent:** Feature-loss complaint → alternative search.
- **Related Kinrows capability:** none (Kinrows has no Alexa integration; note as a gap, not a selling point).
- **Candidate page type:** "AnyList Alexa not working" content.
- **Conversion potential:** Low-medium (niche dependency).

### 3. Deletion / cross-platform (Mac/iOS) inconsistency
- **Problem:** Crossed-off items can't reliably be deleted across devices; confirmation dialogs lie about irreversibility.
- **Quote:** "Can't delete items on both iPhone and MAC. Wish I would've never updated... I selected delete crossed off items and even got a warning saying can't undo it. Well, it never does it so you really can undo it." — Gafdort, 1★, "Used to be good," 2026-08-22
- **User type:** Multi-device households (iPhone + Mac).
- **Intent:** Bug report.
- **Related Kinrows capability:** Lists (iOS-only scope for Kinrows; not a direct feature match but relevant to "reliable list management" positioning).
- **Candidate page type:** none dedicated; folds into general sync-reliability content.
- **Conversion potential:** Low.

### 4. ChatGPT recipe import broke
- **Problem:** "Import from ChatGPT no longer works" — recipe import via AI was a differentiator that regressed.
- **Quote:** "Import from ChatGPT no longer works" — Stainless2000, 3★, "To bad. But one serious flaw," 2026-08-09
- **User type:** Recipe-import power users.
- **Intent:** Bug report.
- **Related Kinrows capability:** Cook — AI recipe ideas from actual pantry inventory (different mechanism, worth contrasting: Kinrows generates from what you have rather than importing external recipes).
- **Candidate page type:** "AnyList vs Kinrows Cook" comparison angle.
- **Conversion potential:** Low-medium.
