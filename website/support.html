Support

Kinrows is a family organizer for iPhone. Email us and we'll get back to you — usually within two business days.

Last updated: August 27, 2026 Email: kinrows@atlasatlantic.co

Get help

Write to kinrows@atlasatlantic.co from the email on your account. Include your username if you remember it, and a short description of what went wrong.

Your account

  • Sign in. Use the email and password you registered with, or Sign in with Apple.
  • Delete your account. In the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. Apple-only accounts confirm with Sign in with Apple. This removes your personal data; shared household items stay with the family.
  • Export a copy. Email us and we'll send a portable copy of your data.

Subscriptions

Concierge is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple. Manage or cancel it in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Restore Purchases is on the in-app paywall if you reinstall.

Report a problem in the household

Long-press a direct message or a household feed post and choose Report. We'll look at it. For anything urgent, email us as well.
