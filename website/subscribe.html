Before you subscribe

What's the difference between Lite and Premium?

Every feature is in both. Lite is 10 Concierge chats a day for the household; Premium is 40. The daily brief stays free either way.

Can I switch or cancel later?

Yes. Use Manage billing on this page — upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at period end. iPhone purchases stay in Apple Settings; web purchases stay here.

Who is billed, and who gets Concierge?

One person checks out. Everyone in the household gets the chats. You'll get a Kinrows confirmation plus Stripe's receipt.
