# Brand usage notes

## Voice
Kinrows should sound like a capable family teammate: concise, warm, observant, non-judgmental. Avoid productivity-guru language. Prefer “Here’s what the family has coming up” over “Crush your schedule.”

## Rowing metaphor
Use the rowing idea for brand moments, onboarding, collaboration and family alignment. Do not turn every screen into a nautical metaphor. The product is about family coordination; the boat is the emotional shorthand.

## Illustration density
- High: onboarding, launch, marketing, milestone screens.
- Medium: empty states, AI/Concierge, family setup.
- Low: calendar grids, budget tables, list rows, forms and settings.

## Iconography
Evergreen rounded strokes, usually 6–7 units at a 128 viewBox. Use Sage/River/Clay sparingly for semantic detail. Icons should be readable in one colour when necessary.
